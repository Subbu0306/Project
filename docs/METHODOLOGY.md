# Scoring Methodology

The client's nine categories are Problem 10%, Market 15%, Product 10%, Business Model 10%, Traction 15%, Competitive Advantage 10%, Financials 15%, Team 10%, and Scalability 5%. Each is scored 0–10 against five anchored bands. Missing evidence scores zero; persuasive wording is not evidence.

**MamakScore = Σ(score × weight) / 10.** The model never calculates it. Client bands are 0–39 Not ready, 40–59 Needs significant validation, 60–74 Potential consideration, 75–89 Strong candidate, and 90–100 Due diligence recommended. Within five points below a boundary, human review is escalated upward without changing the score.

Every category quote must occur in the attributed source after Unicode/whitespace normalization; an altered number fails the whole run. Finance uses the six client formulas in Decimal code. Improvement gain is `(target − current) × weight / 10`.

MamakConfidence formula version 1.0 combines 60% verified evidence coverage and 40% deterministic evidence-marker strength, then subtracts red-flag penalties 2/6/12. Its mandatory status is **PROVISIONAL_NOT_CLIENT_APPROVED**. It is not client policy and is hidden by default.

One structured model call returns judgements, flags, questions and actions. RAG and multi-agent analysis are excluded from this signed MVP.
