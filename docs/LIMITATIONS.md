# Limitations

- Rubric anchors are a PG-S2-29 draft awaiting Dennis Ong's approval.
- MamakConfidence is provisional and hidden by default.
- No human-labelled benchmark or historical MamakScore exists yet.
- The supplied decks contain winners, mostly US companies, no attached scores and no spreadsheets.
- Famous startups risk model-knowledge contamination.
- XLSX parsing uses explicit aliases and cached formula values; ambiguous layouts are reported, not guessed.
- Image-based PDF support needs a configured vision-capable hosted model.
- PPTX, RAG, stage profiles, fine-tuning and multi-agent analysis are outside signed scope.
- Offline mode proves software wiring only. No automated decline is allowed.
